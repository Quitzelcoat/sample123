Resources

Frequently asked questions

Are your services really free?

Most services are free for clients whose household income is at or below 200% of the federal poverty guidelines. For others, we offer low-cost services on a sliding scale.

Will contacting you affect my immigration case?

No. Everything you share with us is confidential and protected. We do not share information with immigration enforcement.

What documents should I bring to a consultation?

Bring a photo ID, your passport, any immigration documents (notices, visas, cards) and any letters you've received from USCIS or immigration court.

How do I recognize notario fraud?

Be cautious of anyone who promises guaranteed results, asks you to sign blank forms, or isn't a licensed attorney or DOJ-accredited representative.
